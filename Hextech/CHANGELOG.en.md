# Hextech changelog (English)

## 0.7.0
- 6 medium trigger augments: Magic Missile, Dawnbringer's Resolve, Flying Sword, Still Here (cheat death once per wave), Sky Strike, Undying (cheat death 3 times per run)

## 0.6.0
- 14 new generic augments: Dive Bomber, Slow Cooker, Restless Restoration, Scopes, two stat conversions, Prismatic Egg and Urf's Champion (quests), Transmute: Gold, Mystic Punch, Auto-cast, and three trade-off prismatics (Glass Cannon, All or Nothing, Heavy)

## 0.5.0
- 4 "feed the carry" augments: give gold to the teammate you support, grow when teammates level up, skill haste for your supported teammate
- 9 teammate augments inspired by Arena (only offered when you have teammates; they use the game's own heal, shield and buff, so teammates don't need mods)

## 0.4.0
- Multiplayer: works when everyone in the room has the same mods (or the host allows it in DBStudio); if that stops being true mid-run, no more picks are offered but existing ones stay
- In multiplayer the pick only appears during the rest phase (the game can't be paused)
- Enemy augments follow the host's setting: enemy HP applies to the whole room, enemy attack only to players with Hextech; teammates with Hextech see what the enemies got

## 0.3.0
- Shotgun hero signature (three tiers): more ammo, two shells per dash, and ammo from crit damage (up to 12)
- New generic augments: Get Excited, Fruitify, Tank Engine, Porcupine, Archmage, Dual Wield
- 9 new hand-drawn icons
- Get Excited no longer triggers on kills that land after the wave has ended

## 0.2.1
- Desert Reaper's signature now has three tiers (Bonk / Bonk! / Bonk Bonk Bonk!) so it can show up in any pick
- Fix: two signature-skill cards with the same name in one pick
- Hand-drawn crayon-style icons for generic and signature augments, paper color by tier

## 0.2.0
- Generic augments inspired by LoL ARAM: Mayhem — every hero can roll them (2 signature-skill cards + 1 generic per pick; heroes without skill cards get 3 generic), including attribute forges that open during the rest phase, and a mana build (Mind to Matter, Manamune, and a prismatic loop card that feeds them back)
- First hand-made signature augment: "Bonk!" for Desert Reaper — the empowered strike splashes to nearby enemies and splash kills grant stacks
- Optional "enemy augments" (config `EnemyAugments`, off by default): enemies get an augment of the same tier each time you pick, and you get one extra reroll per pick

## 0.1.1
- Heroes whose signature skill isn't supported yet (the 10 passives without parameters) now get a notice at the start of the run instead of nothing
