DungeonBrawl Mods 0.32 - Installation and usage
中文说明见 安装说明.txt

Note: the in-game UI of these mods is Chinese only for now.

The package contains 7 mods, each in its own folder under BepInEx\plugins. Delete a folder to remove that mod:
  DBModKit     Shared library required by all the others (main-menu Mod button, multiplayer handshake, Workshop loader)
  SmartSkill   Conditions for auto-cast skills
  SmartMove    Auto-dodge ground telegraphs
  AutoPickup   Auto-pickup
  DamageMeter  Damage meter
  Hextech      Hextech augments
  DBStudio     In-game mod workbench (F9)
Features that haven't been fully verified in game yet are listed under "Not yet tested in game" in RELEASE_NOTES.md.

[Install]
1. In Steam, right-click the game -> Manage -> Browse local files to open the game folder (the one with DungeonBrawl.exe).
2. First install: extract everything from the "full" package into that folder.
   Updating: extract the "plugins" package into that folder and overwrite the old files. Update all mods together, not just one.
   Also using the Workshop: a same-named mod in BepInEx\plugins wins over the Workshop copy. To use the Workshop version, delete that folder in plugins (except DBModKit).
3. Start the game. BepInEx initialises on the first launch, so it may take a little longer.
4. A "Mod" button appears below "Workshop" in the main menu - that means it worked. It lists the installed mods and whether they're running.
   A red "!" on the button means a mod failed to load or needs your confirmation; open it to see why.

[SmartSkill - auto-cast conditions]
The game lets you right-click a skill icon to auto-cast it, but it fires whenever the skill is off cooldown, you have mana and an enemy is nearby.
SmartSkill lets you set conditions per skill, e.g. only heal below 50% HP, only use an AoE when 3+ enemies are in range, prefer bosses.
1. In combat, right-click a skill icon to turn on auto-cast (vanilla feature).
2. A gear appears at the icon's top-right corner; click it to open the settings.
3. Modes:
   Vanilla (原版)            - no change, same as without the mod (default)
   Custom (自定义条件)        - cast only when the ticked conditions are met
   Never (永不自动)           - never auto-cast, even if auto-cast is on
4. Not sure what to pick? Click "Recommend by tags" (按标签推荐) to fill in common conditions for that kind of skill.
Settings are saved to BepInEx\config\SmartSkill.rules.json and shared by all heroes (per skill).
5. Execution-type skills (处决, 点金术, 撕咬) can use "killable enemies >=" (能斩杀的敌人 ≥) so they only fire when they can kill, targeting those enemies first;
   toggle skills such as 献祭 stay on while the conditions hold and turn off when they don't.
You can also review and switch every skill's mode outside combat in DBStudio (F9) -> Mod page.

[SmartMove - auto-dodge]
When you aren't pressing a direction key, it walks you out of red ground telegraphs (enemy skills, traps), with or without auto-battle. F8 toggles it.
Enemy basic attacks always hit once started and can't be dodged; only skills with a red telegraph can.
You can change the hotkey in DBStudio's Mod page. Up to 0.2.0 it was F9, which clashed with DBStudio; since 0.3.0 it's F8, and a key you set yourself is kept.
Settings: BepInEx\config\plge0n.dungeonbrawl.smartmove.cfg

[AutoPickup]
Picks up drops within 5 m during combat (yours, shared ones, and teammates' shared drops) - the same as pressing C for you, no pickup from afar.
Per-category switches: gold, attribute books, skill books, relics, cards, potions and upgrade materials are on by default; runes and story items are off.
When your bag is full, items that take a slot (skill books, potions, ...) are skipped.
Settings: BepInEx\config\plge0n.dungeonbrawl.autopickup.cfg, or DBStudio (F9) -> Mod page.

[DamageMeter]
Shows your damage on the right side of the screen during combat, split by basic attacks, each skill, summons, on-hit effects, damage over time, auras,
thorns, specific equipment and passives, with share and crit rate, plus total, DPS, time, biggest hit, damage taken and kills.
The buttons on the panel switch between "this wave" and "this run".
It only counts damage dealt by you (and your summons) and doesn't change the game, so it works in multiplayer.
In Steam lobbies the "Team" section lists everyone's damage, share and DPS - only for teammates who also have DamageMeter
(damage is computed on each player's own PC, so only their mod can send it). Not supported in LAN mode.
Turn off ShareInLobby if you don't want to share your numbers.
Wave and run summaries are written to BepInEx\LogOutput.log.
Settings (position, size, opacity, rows, hotkey): BepInEx\config\plge0n.dungeonbrawl.damagemeter.cfg, or DBStudio -> Mod page.

[Hextech - augments]
4 picks per run (one at the start, then after the relic pick at roughly 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4 of the waves). Silver, gold and prismatic tiers; prismatic gets more likely later on (max 2 per run).
2 rerolls per run (the extra option; doesn't use the game's own rerolls).
  Signature cards  upgrade your hero's signature skill: damage, cooldown, mana cost, duration, charges, on-cast effects; some heroes have hand-made signature cards
                   (Desert Reaper, Shotgun hero, Goblin King, Bomber, Raiden)
  Generic cards    for every hero, inspired by LoL ARAM: Mayhem and Arena: stats, on-kill triggers, cheat death, extra lightning / sword waves, quests (Prismatic Egg...), trade-offs...
  Teammate cards   only when you have teammates: heals, shields, skill haste for teammates, and "feed the carry" cards (pick the teammate you support)
Heroes with signature cards get 2 of those + 1 generic per pick. Chosen augments are shown to the right of the bag, under your portrait; hover for details. F7 lists them.
Optional enemy augments (config EnemyAugments, off by default): enemies get one of the same tier whenever you pick, and you get an extra reroll per pick.
Multiplayer: follows the rules in [Multiplayer] below; picks only appear during the rest phase (the game can't pause online); enemy augments follow the host's setting.
Settings: BepInEx\config\plge0n.dungeonbrawl.hextech.cfg

[DBStudio - mod workbench]
Press F9 to open it (pauses the game when you're alone). Pages:
  Debug    player stats, skill bar, nearby enemies, run log; test actions (full HP/mana, gold, ...) when alone; "Export" (导出) creates a diagnostic report
  Rules    "when ... if ... then ..." gameplay without code, e.g. "gain gold on crit"
  Augments your own pick-one-of-three upgrades at the end of waves
  Values   change skill, hero and monster numbers; restore vanilla at any time
  Skins    choose a subscribed Workshop model or a local model in BepInEx\skins\ for each hero (cosmetic only);
           in Steam lobbies, teammates who also have the mods see your Workshop skin (if they're subscribed too)
  Mod      settings of every mod (toggles, sliders, hotkeys, ...), saved immediately
Rules, augments and value changes are saved as "gameplay packs" (BepInEx\config\DBStudio\packs\) and follow the multiplayer rules below.

[Multiplayer]
Helper features (auto-cast, dodging, pickup, damage meter) only affect you; teammates don't need the mods.
Gameplay-changing content (custom skills, relics, equipment, content packs, DBStudio gameplay packs) only takes effect when:
  - you're alone in the room;
  - everyone in the room has the mods with exactly the same gameplay content (compared automatically via the Steam lobby);
  - the host ticks "I'm the host: allow gameplay content in this room" under DBStudio (F9) -> Mod page -> DBModKit -> Multiplayer (everyone gets a notice).
Otherwise only cosmetics such as skins stay on for that run; you'll see a notice when entering the dungeon. The state locks when combat starts:
it can only be turned off mid-run, never back on, and anything you already got stays.
LAN games have no Steam lobby, so the mods can't check what others have installed; gameplay content is always disabled there.
Hextech follows the same rule (from 0.31; a player with Hextech switched off doesn't count as having gameplay content). Everyone also needs the same mod versions, so update together.

[Leaderboard]
By the game author's rule, you can't upload to the leaderboard with mods installed. Your results won't be uploaded (you'll see a notice); nothing else is affected.
In someone else's room your build isn't handed to the host for upload either (teammates' results still upload, just without you).
When you host, the whole team's results won't upload. Uninstall the mods if you want to compete on the leaderboard.

[After a game update]
If an update breaks compatibility, a notice at the top of the screen says "DBModKit ... 不兼容" (incompatible) and the mods are disabled.
They won't change anything, the game plays normally; wait for a mod update.

[Uninstall]
A single mod: delete its folder under BepInEx\plugins in the game folder (e.g. BepInEx\plugins\SmartMove).
DBModKit is the shared library every other mod needs; removing it stops all of them.
To remove BepInEx as well, also delete: the BepInEx folder, winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini, changelog.txt, .doorstop_version.

[Reporting problems]
Press F9 to open DBStudio -> Debug page -> "Export" (导出), and send the generated BepInEx\DBModKit\report-<time>.txt to the author;
BepInEx\LogOutput.log helps too.
If you can reproduce it, first set FileLevel to Debug under Mod page -> DBModKit, then press Ctrl+F10 at the moment it happens to add a marker.
